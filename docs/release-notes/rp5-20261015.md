## Device

Retroid Pocket 5 (Snapdragon 865), both screen revisions. Boots from SD next to Android; an internal install is optional.

This release rebuilds the audio stack and fixes docked use. DRAFT for Cliff's rewrite; the technical record is tools/dock/results-rp5-2026-10-15.md and docs/audio-architecture-rp5-2026-10-15.md.

## What works

- Steam Big Picture, controls, rumble, Wi-Fi, sleep and wake.
- Speakers and the headphone jack. The volume slider is linear: 100 % is as loud as the amplifiers go, 50 % is a comfortable level, and the device starts at 50 %.
- The screen runs at 60 Hz by default and switches to 120 Hz when a game's frame limit asks for it (per-game "120 Hz Screen" toggle in Pocknix Control).
- Games keep to the fast cores; shader compiling uses the others.
- Steam's Desktop Mode has an on-screen keyboard (icon on the panel).
- USB-C docks: video on the external screen (1080p, 60 Hz) and audio over the dock's HDMI or DisplayPort. Audio follows the dock by itself: plug in and sound moves to the screen, unplug and it returns to the speakers, including the first sound after plugging in. Booting with a dock attached works.
- Sleep on a dock: the device sleeps and wakes on the dock with the picture, charging and the dock's ethernet coming back; tested over a night.
- Wi-Fi stays reachable while a dock with ethernet is attached, and after the dock is removed.
- Game intro videos in Unity titles that ship them as H.264/VP9/Theora have their decoders present (Hollow Knight still skips its intro; that one is a different format).

## Known issues

- Heavy 3D games run at 10 to 30 fps and the device gets hot. See the [games page](../games-rp5.md) for numbers and settings that help.
- DirectX 12 games do not run on this GPU.
- Native Linux games cannot run; every game goes through Proton.
- Holodor runs Valve's ARM64 Steam client, which Valve ships for its own hardware and does not support elsewhere. Problems in the client itself may stay unfixed.
- Steam needs free space to install games; installing several at once on a nearly full drive can stall the device until it is rebooted.
- Some games' first launch may sit on a loading screen for a few minutes (Dark Souls Remastered: about two).
- At 100 % the speakers are at the amplifiers' limit; loud game mixes clip there. Android plays about 5 dB louder through its speaker-protection processing, which Holodor does not have yet.
- With some HDMI dongles the external screen comes up at 640x480 and without audio because the dongle's identification could not be read. Unplug and plug the dock again.
- The dock's ethernet occasionally comes back at USB 2.0 speed after a wake (about 300 Mbit/s instead of gigabit). Replug the dock to restore it.
