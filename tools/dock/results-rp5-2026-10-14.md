Result: DisplayPort works in BOTH plug orientations on BOTH docks. The 2026-10-12 "reversed plug = hardware
limit" was wrong; the AUX pair goes through a GPIO analog switch (enable tlmm 9 active low, select tlmm 8 =
reversed), taken from the live Android device tree. On kernel 25 tlmm 9 was an undriven input (switch off), so
24/25 had no DP in either orientation.
