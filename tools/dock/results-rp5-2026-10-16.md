## 10:xx JST Cliff: sleep drain. Exited Vampire Survivors, slept at 31 %, woke 6 h later at 15 %.
- = 16 % / 6.4 h = 2.5 %/h; battery charge_full_design 3850 mAh -> ~96 mA average in "sleep". A real suspend should sit under 1 %/h.
- journal: ONE suspend 18:41:33 -> 01:05:52 UTC (6 h 24 min), suspend_stats success=10 fail=0, no cycling, 2 kernel lines inside the window (the wifi radio-off audit + "Filesystems sync").
- GROUND TRUTH (qcom_stats, /sys/kernel/debug/qcom_stats): aosd=0 cxsd=0 ddr=0 over the whole 13 h boot INCLUDING the 6.4 h suspend -> the SoC never entered its low-power modes; adsp Count=1 (slept once, 4 ms, during the first suspend of the boot), cdsp sleeps fine. CPU cluster domain (pm_genpd power-domain-cpu-cluster0) S2idle entries: one per suspend; per-CPU s2idle counters move -> the CPUs power-collapse in s2idle, the SoC does not follow.
- blocker found and measured: bsp-sm8250's 99-wcd938x-nosleep.rules (udev, control=on for both wcd938x SoundWire halves; copied verbatim from ROCKNIX devices/SM8250, 07-11, "to fix jack detection", no measurement in either tree) holds the codec active -> both SoundWire masters + RX/TX macros active -> lpass-rx/tx-mclk enabled 24/7. Lifting it live (control=auto): the whole chain suspends in < 8 s, LPASS clocks off. NOT the DP-switcher polling (that was the ~1 % core thing, removed 10-15).
- cycle 1 (chain suspended, 2-min suspend via rtcwake alarm + systemctl suspend): aosd/cxsd/ddr still 0, adsp still 1 -> the codec rule is not sufficient; more blockers (wifi PCIe link active at 5 GT/s, USB dwc3 active with wakeup, ADSP never sleeping).
- method notes: `rtcwake -m mem` bypasses systemd-sleep (no hooks); use `rtcwake -m no -s N; systemctl suspend [-i]`. IRQ/wakeup snapshots must be taken by sleep.d hooks at the edges (pre 999 / post 000); ssh-side snapshots include ~25 s of awake time and are useless. The device fell into Steam's idle sleep twice during the block because no inhibitor was held (my miss, again); block = sleeptest-inhibit + `systemctl suspend -i`.
- side finding: bsp-common's sleep.d post/005-cpuidle-state1 forces cpu0 state1 disable=1 after EVERY resume on every device, but the GMU quirk it serves is SM8550-only (bsp-sm8250 PKGBUILD note): on the RP5 cpu0 loses its power-collapse state while awake after the first sleep (cpu0 cpu-sleep-0-0: 18 s in 13 h). Fix candidate: the post hook restores the pre-sleep value instead of forcing 1.
- reference checks: steamed-noodle (OnePlus 8 Steam port) uses a userspace standby, no kernel suspend, no drain numbers; ROCKNIX ships the same codec rule; ROCKNIX control card test declined by Cliff for now.
